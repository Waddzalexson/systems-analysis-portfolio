# Fictional Case Study: Procurement Workflow Improvement

> This is a fictional demonstration created for portfolio purposes. It does not describe a real client or disclose client information.

## Organisation

Apex Services Ltd, a fictional mid-sized services company with multiple departments and a central finance team.

## Business problem

Procurement requests are submitted through email and followed up through calls and spreadsheets. Staff cannot easily see where a request is, why it is delayed, which approvals are outstanding, or whether the final purchase matched the approved request.

## Current-state issues

- request information is inconsistent
- approvals are difficult to trace
- supporting quotations are stored in different places
- budget checks may happen late
- requesters repeatedly contact finance for status updates
- supplier selection decisions are not consistently documented
- purchase orders, receipts and invoices are difficult to reconcile as one transaction trail

## Objective

Create a controlled digital workflow that gives each request a clear owner, status, approval history and supporting evidence from request through payment preparation.

## Stakeholders

- Requester
- Department Head
- Procurement Officer
- Finance Officer
- Approver
- Receiving Officer
- System Administrator

## Proposed future-state workflow

1. Requester creates a purchase requisition.
2. System validates required fields and supporting information.
3. Department Head reviews the business need.
4. Finance confirms budget availability where required.
5. Procurement collects quotations and records the evaluation.
6. Appropriate approver reviews the recommended supplier.
7. Approved request generates or records a purchase order.
8. Receiving Officer confirms delivery or service completion.
9. Finance matches request, approval, purchase order, receipt and invoice.
10. Payment preparation is recorded and the request is closed.

## Key controls

- mandatory fields before submission
- role-based access
- approval limits
- quotation and supplier evidence
- no purchase order before required approvals
- receipt confirmation before payment preparation
- timestamped status and approval history
- controlled cancellation and rejection reasons

## Expected business value

- clearer accountability
- fewer status follow-ups
- more consistent documentation
- stronger audit trail
- better visibility of outstanding requests
- easier reporting on procurement activity

## Deliverables demonstrated in this repository

- business requirements document
- user stories and acceptance criteria

- phased implementation plan
